Strange Family Vineyards

A Family Obsession

We are a family bound to one stretch of cool, wind-raked hillside in the Sta. Rita Hills — where the Pacific fog lingers past noon and the fruit ripens slow and bright.

Every bottle carries the patience of the place: méthode champenoise sparkling, restrained Pinot Noir, taut Chardonnay, and a cold-climate Syrah that surprises.
